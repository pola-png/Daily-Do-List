Privacy Policy for Daily Do List

Effective Date: November 25, 2026  |  App: Daily Do List

Summary: Daily Do List is an offline-first, local-only task manager. We do not collect, store on external servers, sell, or share any of your personal data.

1. Information We Collect

Daily Do List does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information, usage analytics, device identifiers, location data, or crash reports on external servers.

  • No Account Required: You do not need to register or sign in to use the app.
  • No Ads or Tracking: The app contains no third-party advertising SDKs, analytics trackers, or behavioral profiling tools.

2. Local Data Storage

All tasks, completion statuses, and app data you create in Daily Do List are stored exclusively on your device using local device storage. Your tasks never leave your device unless you explicitly choose to back up your device using your operating system's built-in backup features.

3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

Because we do not collect any user data, we do not share, sell, rent, or disclose any personal information to third parties.

4. Permissions

Daily Do List is designed with minimal permissions and does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, location, or files.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Your task data remains on your device for as long as you keep it in the app. You can edit or delete individual tasks at any time inside the app. Uninstalling Daily Do List or clearing the app's storage in your device settings permanently removes all locally stored data.

6. Children's Privacy

Daily Do List does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If we make changes to this Privacy Policy in future updates (for example, if we add optional cloud sync), we will update the "Effective Date" at the top of this page and publish the updated policy at this URL before those changes take effect.
